Property business concept · Self-initiated demonstration

Aurelle.
Homes with perspective.

A dedicated property website that makes a collection of homes easy to explore, compare and revisit. A new visual identity, designed for the buyer’s journey.

Explore Aurelle ↗

01 / The brief

A business website.
A distinct identity.

Create a fictional luxury property brand with warm ivory, charcoal and bronze, editorial type and large architectural photography. The website serves buyers rather than presenting a developer portfolio.

02 / The experience

From discovery
to a closer look.

Eight generated property pages share one dataset. Visitors filter by location, type, bedrooms and price, sort results, browse galleries, and save homes in their browser. A validated enquiry form demonstrates the viewing journey.

03 / The build

Small foundations.
Useful interactions.

Static HTML, CSS and native JavaScript, with no runtime framework or paid service. Local WebP image sizes, locally hosted fonts, semantic controls, keyboard-accessible galleries and reduced-motion support keep the experience focused.

This is self-initiated demonstration work, not a commissioned client website. All homes, specifications and prices are fictional. Licensed photographs are architectural references. The enquiry preview sends no messages, books no viewings and stores no personal information. No commercial results or measured performance claims are asserted.
