Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Anjali Kadian, timelines, pricing, and technical deliverables.

How much does a custom website cost? expand_more

Focused single-page builds start at $200 – $300. Multi-page marketing sites start at $1,500, and custom web applications or 3D work start at $3,500. I confirm the exact scope and fixed price before we begin. Domain, hosting, and ongoing services are separate.

How long does it take to build and launch? expand_more

A single-page build typically takes 3 to 5 business days after scope, content, and deposit are confirmed. Larger projects get their own timeline in the proposal.

Do I own the code and design files after launch? expand_more

You receive the source code and design assets created for your project, plus a handover for your deployment. Any third-party hosting, domain, or software costs are identified in the proposal.

Why Next.js instead of WordPress, Wix, or Webflow? expand_more

WordPress and Webflow websites can become dependent on plugins and platform constraints as they grow. Next.js gives teams a strong foundation for fast rendering, technical SEO, accessibility, and complete design freedom—measured on the actual project rather than promised in advance.

Can you add 3D animations to my existing website? expand_more

Yes. I can scope a 3D component for an existing Next.js, React, or static website. I test the interaction on mobile and provide a reduced-motion fallback when it is appropriate.
