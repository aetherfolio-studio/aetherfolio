Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Anish Kadian, timelines, pricing, and technical deliverables.

How much does a custom website cost? expand_more

Focused single-page pilot builds start at $200 – $300 for early founders. Full custom marketing websites start at $1,500, and complex web applications or interactive 3D WebGL experiences start at $3,500. Every project has a clear, fixed-price quote with zero surprise fees.

How long does it take to build and launch? expand_more

A single-page pilot build typically launches in 3 to 5 business days. A complete custom marketing site takes 2 to 3 weeks, and full-stack web applications take 4 to 6 weeks from approved scope to live launch.

Do I own the code and design files after launch? expand_more

Yes, 100%. You receive full ownership of the GitHub repository, source code, design assets, and deployment keys. There are no recurring platform lock-ins or license fees. You own everything outright.

Why Next.js instead of WordPress, Wix, or Webflow? expand_more

WordPress and Webflow websites often slow down over time, become dependent on fragile plugins, and limit your custom design. Next.js delivers sub-second load speeds, 100/100 Google Core Web Vitals, enterprise security, and complete design freedom without platform limits.

Can you add 3D animations to my existing website? expand_more

Yes! I build lightweight, GPU-accelerated Three.js and WebGL components that integrate directly into existing Next.js, React, or static websites. They look stunning without draining battery or slowing down mobile devices.
